Customer Success Manager — B2B SaaS (LATAM)

We are looking for a Customer Success Manager to support our enterprise customer
portfolio across Latin America. We are a growing SaaS startup and the CS team is key
to retention and account expansion.

Responsibilities:
- Manage a portfolio of 25-30 enterprise accounts, from onboarding to renewal.
- Design and execute per-customer success plans, with adoption and account-health metrics.
- Detect expansion opportunities (upsell / cross-sell) and coordinate with Sales.
- Lead QBRs (Quarterly Business Reviews) with management-level stakeholders.
- Work with Product, relaying customer feedback in a structured way.
- Document processes and improve the team's playbooks.

Hard requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in Customer Success, Account Management or similar roles in B2B SaaS.
- Advanced English (C1) to work with international teams and customers.
- Experience with a CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce or similar).

Nice to have:
- Experience leading or mentoring other CSMs.
- Familiarity with CS tools (Gainsight, Vitally, Planhat).
- Startup or scale-up experience.
- Basic SQL or data analysis skills.
